## 3. What this is not

- Not 500 labels. The tool generates prompts; it does not answer, score or
  adjudicate them.
- Not a frozen packet. No file is committed as the 500-prompt set; the frame
  is reproducible from the seed and the input hashes, and freezes only after
  the owner chooses §4 A or B.
- Not evidence of coverage or quality. It is a sampling measurement.
- Not a change to the 500-prompt gate status: **BLOCKED** until 500 labeled
  rows exist.

## 4. The decision this measurement informs

**Decided 2026-11-06: option A (GF path at scale).** The owner chose to keep
the gate as an M7/GF gate: slot coverage comes first (S2 signed as written,
S3 signed as topic groups — group 1 = 116 rows, then group 2 = 91), and the
500-prompt frame is redrawn as a GF-enriched frame after coverage lands. The
uniform frame below stays as the recorded baseline and is **not** relabeled or
renamed; option B (rename as a text-path gate) was not chosen.

The pilot protocol names two honest readings:

| option | what it measures | cost | what it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — GF path at scale** | M7 pipeline | slot coverage + labeling | finish slot coverage (B, then C), then draw a GF-enriched frame |
| **B — text path at scale** | the unmapped text path | ~17 h labeling | explicitly rename the gate; do not call it M7 |

The measured 5/500 GF share is the reason A cannot be done by relabeling the
uniform frame: even after the `oov-005` fix, a uniform draw still measures the
text path. If the goal is M7, the slot batch (S2/S3) comes first; if the goal
is text-path quality, the gate must say so in its name and its report.

**Consequence of A (signed 2026-11-06):** the next frame is not this one. Once
slot coverage lands (group 1 then group 2), a GF-enriched frame is drawn and
frozen, and only that packet can be labeled as the 500-prompt M7 gate. Until
then the 500-prompt gate stays **BLOCKED** and this document remains a
baseline measurement, not a packet.

## 6. GF-enriched mode — measured 2026-11-09/10, no frame frozen

**Owner decision 2026-11-09**: build the tooling first, **do not freeze** a
frame. The gate stays BLOCKED.

`--enriched` crosses the GF-covered topics with a table of *verified*
definitional carriers (prefixes of «что такое <topic>?»). The carrier space is
prefix-only **by construction**: `IntentDefine` extracts the topic after the
first literal «что такое» (`Semantic/Intent/Classifier.hs`,
`extractTopicAfter`), so any trailing material becomes part of the topic and
the turn leaves the GF path.

### Carrier verification contract

A carrier enters `CARRIERS` only after a runtime probe shows it keeps the
`CMDefine` family **and** the same topic as the bare shape. The contract is
family+topic, **not** predicate: a carrier changes the utterance features,
hence the `Field`, and the per-predicate selector may then pick a different
predicate for the same topic. Measured example (2026-11-09):

| carrier | topic | path | atmosphere arousal | emitted predicate |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| «что такое свобода?» | свобода | `covered_exact` / `exact` | 0.15 | свобода предполагает возможность выбора |
| «мне нужно понять, что такое свобода?» | свобода | `covered_exact` / `exact` | 0.71 | свобода выражает автономию человека |

So an enriched frame is **not** a replication design: each row is its own
turn with its own Field state. It measures the GF path under phrasing
variation, which is what the carrier dimension can honestly claim.

### Measured ceiling

12 carriers are verified (2026-11-09): `bare`, `скажи, `, `объясни, `, `а `,
`интересно, `, `кратко: `, `определи, `, `поясни, `, `ответь, `,
`помоги понять, `, `мне нужно понять, `, `расскажи, `. All 12 keep `CMDefine`
and the topic; 11 of 12 also kept the bare predicate, the exception being the
`мне нужно понять, ` row above.

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| GF-covered topics | 30 |
| verified carriers | 12 |
| **enriched ceiling** | **360** |
| requested 500 | **refused loudly** (exit 1) |
| `--max` frame | 360 rows, 30 distinct topics, all GF-mapped |

**A 500-prompt GF frame cannot be built from the current corpus.** The ceiling
is 360, and the carrier dimension is nearly saturated: the 12 prefixes are
already stylistically redundant, and one of them perturbs the Field enough to
change the content. Reaching 500 would need 17 verified carriers (5 more
probes, ~6 min of runtime each) and would be 500 prompts about 30 topics.

### Two facts that bound what such a frame can claim

1. **The 30 GF-covered topics are exactly the 30 pilot `anchor` topics**
   (verified: set difference is empty in both directions), and all 30 are
   single-word. The enriched frame is therefore not an independent sample of
   the population — it is the pilot's own anchor set crossed with carriers.
2. **Cost at current latency**: ~70 s per prompt (bootstrap ~23 s + turn
   ~47 s), so 500 prompts ≈ 11 h of wall clock, before the ~17 h of two-rater
   labeling. The 360-row ceiling is ≈ 7 h.

```bash

```

The probe script itself is **not** committed (same gap as the M7 batch
harness): the command above is the reproducible form.
