# Phase 10 Long-Session Corpus Observations

Generated by the §4 calibration addendum run of the long-session corpus harness
(`docs/post-phase-10-roadmap-closure-spec.md` §4.1).

## 1. Summary

The synthetic long-session fixtures were replayed through `runTurnInSession` with
`QXFX0_ESSENCE_COMMITMENT_ENABLED=1`.  Extended per-turn logging captured
`etAngstLevel`, `etConatusFloor`, `ewConatusScalar` of the latest witness, and
commitment state after every turn.

**Key finding: no `EssenceCommitted` events were observed in any fixture.**
Both angst and conatus-floor dynamics were flat across the entire corpus.

## 6. Root-cause analysis

Two independent pathologies block commitment:

### 6.1 Angst never accrues

`witness` only increases `etAngstLevel` when the `ReconcileRule` is
`RuleHolisticAdvantage` or `RuleFormalAdvantage` **and**
`dtDivergence >= emAngstAccrualDivergenceFloor` (currently 0.5).

Across all 70 turns of all fixtures, the runtime pipeline produced angst
accrual conditions **zero times**.  The `etAngstLevel` remained at its
`emptyTrajectory` seed of `0.0` for every turn.

### 6.2 Conatus erosion never triggers

`shouldCommit` tests `TriggerConatusErosion` when **all** of the last
`emConatusFloorWindow` (8) witnesses have `ewConatusScalar < emConatusStructuralFloor`
(currently 0.5).

The observed `ewConatusScalar` values were ~14.2–15.0, well above 0.5.
Because `etConatusFloor = min previousFloor ceScalar` and `ceScalar` is
consistently > 1.0, the floor stayed at its seed value of `1.0` for every
turn.  No window of 8 sub-floor witnesses ever existed.

## 7. Calibration implications

### 7.1 Conatus floor — unit-mismatch corrected

The observed `ewConatusScalar ∈ [14.2, 15.0]` revealed a fundamental
unit mismatch: `computeConatusEnergy` in `QxFx0.Self.Conatus` produces
a log-scale unbounded scalar, not a `[0, 1]` value. The Phase 9 default
`emConatusStructuralFloor = 0.5` was calibrated against
`arbitraryUnitDouble` generators (which emit `[0, 1]`), not against
the production runtime codomain.

**Correction applied:** `emConatusStructuralFloor` raised to `7.0`
(≈ half of observed healthy `ceScalar`). The Phase 9 value is preserved
as `phase9EssenceModulation` for regression locks. See ADR-0012 §15.1.

### 7.2 Angst parameters — deferred

Because the deliberation pipeline never emitted `Rule*Advantage` with
`dtDivergence ≥ 0.5`, no angst accrual occurred. Adjusting angst-side
thresholds or rates within the §4.2 allowed range (factor of 2)
**cannot produce a commitment** without upstream rule divergence.

Angst-side calibration (`emAngstCommitmentThreshold`,
`emAngstAccrualRate`, `emAngstDecayRate`,
`emAngstAccrualDivergenceFloor`) remains at Phase 9 values.
Future calibration requires production trace ingestion or a
test-only `Deliberation` injection hook. See ADR-0012 §15.2.

## 8. Resolution status

- `emConatusStructuralFloor`: **Corrected** (0.5 → 7.0).
- Angst-side parameters: **Deferred** — upstream blockage.
- `phase9EssenceModulation` / `defaultEssenceModulation` split: **Landed**.
- ADR-0012 §15: **Appended** with both findings.
