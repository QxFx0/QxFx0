NON-AUTHORITY (eval-only)
=========================

Everything in this directory is eval/report tooling. It is not on the runtime
authority path and must never be invoked by the runtime to produce or validate
a user-visible surface.

Classification and retention rule:
docs/closure/PYTHON_SUPPLIER_ALLOWLIST.md, section 3 item 3
(eval-only Python lives in scripts/eval/ with a NON_AUTHORITY marker).

The frozen runtime authority remains the Haskell library and `qxfx0-main`.
