# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want QxFx0 to be an open, technical, and respectful collaboration space.
Participants are expected to communicate directly and professionally, including
when disagreeing on architecture or implementation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in technical discussions.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Provide evidence for claims when possible (logs, paths, tests).
- Keep discussions focused on repository goals and constraints.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Deliberate disruption of review and collaboration processes.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block access
for conduct violations.

## Reporting

Use the security/reporting channel in `SECURITY.md` for confidential reports.
