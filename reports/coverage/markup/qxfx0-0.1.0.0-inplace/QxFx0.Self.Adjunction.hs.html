   11 A typed, pure realisation of the @Holistic ⊣ Formal@ adjunction

   14 
   15 == What this module is
   16 
   17 The right-hemispheric (\"holistic\") and left-hemispheric (\"formal\")
   18 modes of operation live as two parameterised types,
   19 
   20 @
   21 'Holistic' a = (a, 'Field')      -- value perceived together with its field
   22 'Formal'   a = 'Field' -> a      -- value as a strategy across fields
   23 @
   24 
   25 The pair is the textbook product–exponential adjunction
   26 \(- \times s \dashv s \to -\) with \(s = \mathrm{Field}\), and it
   27 is the standard /currying/ isomorphism
   28 
   29 @
   30 ('Holistic' a -> b)  ≅  (a -> 'Formal' b)
   31 @
   32 
   33 witnessed by 'leftAdjunct' and 'rightAdjunct'. The unit and counit
   34 of the adjunction are forced by the types up to 'Functor' action;
   35 neither requires a designated zero field, monoid structure, or any
   36 choice beyond what the types determine.
   37 
   38 == What this module is /not/
   39 
   40 This module is the algebraic /spine/ of the dual-mode story. It
   41 does /not/ implement any holistic computation, does /not/ touch
   42 the turn pipeline, and does /not/ commit to a final shape of
   43 'Field' (which is intentionally a stub here and grows in Phase 4).
   44 Phase 4–5 work re-shapes call sites in @Core.Intuition@,
   45 @Core.ConsciousnessLoop@, and @RouteEffects@ in terms of these
   46 types; this module only provides the algebra those phases will
   47 quote from.
   48 
   49 == Triangle identities
   50 
   51 The adjunction is required to satisfy
   52 
   53 @
   54 counit . fmap unit       ≡ id   -- on 'Holistic' (left triangle)
   55 fmap counit . unit       ≡ id   -- on 'Formal'   (right triangle, pointwise)
   56 @
   57 
   58 Both hold by direct calculation on the concrete instance below;
   59 both are also verified by the @Test.Suite.SelfAdjunction@
   60 property suite.

   77 -- | Categorical adjunction @l ⊣ r@: a pair of functors with a
   78 -- natural hom-set isomorphism witnessed by @leftAdjunct@ and
   79 -- @rightAdjunct@, equivalently by the 'unit' and 'counit' natural
   80 -- transformations.
   81 --
   82 -- Default implementations express @leftAdjunct@/@rightAdjunct@ in
   83 -- terms of @unit@/@counit@; instances may override them for
   84 -- efficiency but the laws (the triangle identities and
   85 -- naturality) must hold either way.
   86 class (Functor l, Functor r) => Adjunction l r | l -> r, r -> l where
   87   -- | @η : 1 ⇒ r ∘ l@. Lifts a value into the round-trip
   88   -- @r (l a)@.
   89   unit         :: a -> r (l a)
   90 
   91   -- | @ε : l ∘ r ⇒ 1@. Collapses a round-trip @l (r a)@ back to
   92   -- @a@.
   93   counit       :: l (r a) -> a
   94 
   95   -- | The forward direction of the hom-set isomorphism
   96   -- @Hom(l a, b) ≅ Hom(a, r b)@.
   97   --
   98   -- __Status (Phase A consolidation):__ documentation-only; production
   99   -- call sites land in Phase 8 (deliberation framework, see ROADMAP.md).
  100   leftAdjunct  :: (l a -> b) -> (a -> r b)
  101   leftAdjunct g a = fmap g (unit a)
  102 
  103   -- | The reverse direction of the hom-set isomorphism
  104   -- @Hom(l a, b) ≅ Hom(a, r b)@.
  105   rightAdjunct :: (a -> r b) -> (l a -> b)
  106   rightAdjunct k la = counit (fmap k la)
  107 
  108 -- | Right-hemispheric mode: a value perceived /with/ its field,
  109 -- inseparable from its ground.
  110 --
  111 -- The left adjoint of the adjunction. As a functor, 'Holistic' is
  112 -- the product functor @(- × Field)@; @fmap@ acts on the value and
  113 -- leaves the field untouched.
  114 newtype Holistic a = Holistic { runHolistic :: (a, Field) }
  115   deriving stock (Eq, Show, Functor)
  116 
  117 -- | Left-hemispheric mode: a value as a /strategy/ across fields,
  118 -- a context-respecting commitment that is re-evaluable in any
  119 -- field.
  120 --
  121 -- The right adjoint of the adjunction. As a functor, 'Formal' is
  122 -- the exponential @(Field -> -)@; @fmap@ post-composes with the
  123 -- function.
  124 newtype Formal a = Formal { runFormal :: Field -> a }
  125   deriving stock (Functor)
  126 
  127 -- | The adjunction @Holistic ⊣ Formal@. The unit and counit are
  128 -- forced by the types up to 'Functor' action and require no
  129 -- additional structure on 'Field'.
  130 instance Adjunction Holistic Formal where
  131   unit a                            = Formal (\fd -> Holistic (a, fd))
  132   counit (Holistic (Formal f, fd))  = f fd
  133 
  134 -- | Sharpen: extract the value from a holistic observation,
  135 -- forgetting the field. The dual of 'rebroaden' at the value
  136 -- level; together they witness the basic correspondence
  137 --
  138 -- @groundIn (Holistic (a, fd)) ≡ runFormal (rebroaden a) fd ≡ a@
  139 --
  140 -- __Status (Phase A consolidation):__ documentation-only; production
  141 -- call sites land in Phase 8 (deliberation framework, see ROADMAP.md).
  142 groundIn :: Holistic a -> a
  143 groundIn (Holistic (a, _fd)) = a
  144 
  145 -- | Broaden: lift a bare value to a formal strategy that ignores
  146 -- the field on which it is evaluated.
  147 --
  148 -- __Status (Phase A consolidation):__ documentation-only; production
  149 -- call sites land in Phase 8 (deliberation framework, see ROADMAP.md).
  150 rebroaden :: a -> Formal a
  151 rebroaden a = Formal (\_fd -> a)
  152 
  153 -- | Evaluate a formal commitment under a probe field without
  154 -- baking it into a particular call site.
  155 --
  156 -- This is just 'runFormal'; the alias exists to mark observational
  157 -- (diagnostic) uses distinct from genuine grounding. Production
  158 -- code should generally go through 'rightAdjunct' rather than call
  159 -- 'probe' directly.
  160 --
  161 -- __Status (Phase A consolidation):__ documentation-only; production
  162 -- call sites land in Phase 8 (deliberation framework, see ROADMAP.md).
  163 probe :: Formal a -> Field -> a
  164 probe = runFormal
