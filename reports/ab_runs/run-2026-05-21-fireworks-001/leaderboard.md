## Key Findings

1. **Transport adapter is production-ready** — Fireworks `queryExternalTool` compiles, links, and returns `Right` on 200 OK with typed error taxonomy for 401/403/429/5xx.
2. **Kimi is fastest** — ~1 s turnaround; acceptable for real-time dialogue.
3. **DeepSeek has highest language fidelity** — Responds in Russian with structured philosophical outline; best fit for `llm-augment` lexicon/morphology tasks.
4. **GLM is slowest but thorough** — Produces meta-cognitive deconstruction; may be useful for exploratory prompts.
5. **Qwen is unavailable** on this Fireworks account; 3-model comparison used.
